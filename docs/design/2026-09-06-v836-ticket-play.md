## Friction and judgment limits

- Refusal is not one thing:11 was a safe but unattractive long Lover packet;32–33 were full cabin;17–19 and44–46 were constrained by chosen earlier commitments. A safe alternative such as Officer24/25 was deliberately declined. Do not call every skip forced.
- Repeated shortfall creates inactivity even when valuable links are visible: rejected Coach27, Celebrity45 and Coach46 because of power. Local tickets improved opportunities but did not eliminate this experience.
- I misbudgeted47–50 as empty coasting after Coach/Tourist delivery. Empty cabin requires a rider, which the disabled button exposed at47. The rule already exists in tests; this is a player comprehension issue, not a newly discovered engine defect.
- I also incorrectly assumed shop charging occurs after the survival check. Actual49→50 survived at2 power because2−5+5=2. Forecast `no change` was CORRECT. That error was corrected publicly and in the append-only log; do not introduce a regression by changing settlement order.
- Drunk21–23 link was planned as high-band fare, but departure at4 meant no high premium despite arriving in high agitation. Revised card copy now names departure timing explicitly.
- Resource rail tweens briefly showed107 instead of fixed-header111 at shop10,111 instead of114 at40. Use settled shop headers for accounting. Animation itself is not an accounting bug.
- Final53 Bomb was a deliberate profitable risk:19 coins for2 energy, spending the coverage buffer and relying on a future positive-energy/free rider. No rescue appeared. Skipping that Bomb would change downstream choices and RNG; this game alone does not prove a counterfactual win. Death is partly attributable to this decision, not solely the game economy.
